## Q1: Why is the plugin market empty and unusable?

The plugin market has been launched since Android version 1.0.1. If it is still blank, please check your network.

## Q2: What is the "Calculate Unlock Code" function for?

![img](./img/function/q2-1.png)

This function is used when you forget your wristband password. Please do not try it casually. After obtaining and entering the unlock code, the wristband will be restored to factory settings.
